Why this hub exists

Grand Theft Auto VI is scheduled for Thursday, 19 November 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar has not (as of this site’s last update) published one single worldwide unlock clock time, and a PC version has not been announced. Search traffic around “GTA 6 countdown”, “unlock time India”, and “which edition to buy” spikes hard near launch — this site answers those jobs with small, honest tools.

We do not host trailers, screenshots, or Rockstar artwork. Facts that are still fuzzy — prices, edition names, file sizes, exact unlock hours — live in editable /data files and show an unconfirmed label in the UI. Update config.js when Rockstar posts the official clock time.
