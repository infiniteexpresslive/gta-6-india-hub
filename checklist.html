Why use a launch checklist?

Launch week chaos is usually boring: wrong account signed in, not enough free SSD space, payment method expired, or downloading overnight with no idea when the store will unlock. A short GTA 6 launch checklist keeps those failures off the critical path so you can focus on actually starting the game.

Items here are generic best practices — not Rockstar support docs. Install size and online multiplayer subscription rules should be verified on the official PlayStation, Xbox, or Rockstar pages closer to 19 November 2026. Edit data/checklist.json if you want to add region-specific steps (for example UPI payment reminders for Indian storefronts).

Pair this list with the countdown / timezone tool and the console comparison so storage and unlock expectations stay honest while details remain unconfirmed.
