    How to read this countdown

    Search interest in “GTA 6 countdown”, “unlock time India”, and “release time in my timezone” peaks in the final week before launch. This page exists to answer those queries without inventing Rockstar announcements. The default mode is local-midnight: we treat 00:00 on 19 November 2026 in your selected timezone as the working unlock instant. That is a convenience assumption, not an official statement.

    If Rockstar later posts a single global unlock (for example a fixed UTC time), change unlockMode to fixed-utc, set launchDateUtc, and set showUnlockUnconfirmedNotice to false in config.js. Until then, treat every clock reading as provisional and double-check Newswire or your platform store on launch day.

    A PC release has not been announced here. When the configured date is in the past, the widget shows It’s out! based on your local calculation — actual store unlock may still differ by region.

    FAQ

    When does GTA 6 release?

    Thursday, 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, per Rockstar’s announced date. Exact clock time remains unconfirmed on this fan site.

    What about unlock time in India?

    Use Asia/Kolkata in the dropdown. Until Rockstar confirms, we show local midnight IST on launch day as a placeholder.

    Can I trust the city list?

    City rows use the same assumption as the converter — helpful for comparison, not a substitute for official unlock posts.

    Why does it say “It’s out!”?

    Your device clock is past the configured launch instant for the selected timezone. Store availability can still lag.
