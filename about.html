About

About UnlockIST

UnlockIST (brand configurable to Ready for VI or GTA6 Prep India) is a lightweight, unofficial fan utility site for India launch week: IST countdown, download-time math, rupee cost checklist, day planner, and edition quiz. Plain HTML/CSS/JS — drag to Vercel or Netlify.

What we are not

Not Rockstar, Take-Two, PlayStation, or Xbox. No official logos, trailers, screenshots, proprietary fonts, or music. Visuals are original CSS and SVG.

Facts vs placeholders

Confirmed calendar target used here: Thursday, 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Exact unlock clock time, INR prices, and install size are treated as unconfirmed until you verify official sources. PC is not announced on this site’s last update.

Monetization

AdSense-ready slots (hidden until configured), affiliate check-price links with disclosure, optional analytics. See privacy.

Contact

Set contactEmail in config.js. Built for search traffic around launch — solo indie maintenance.
