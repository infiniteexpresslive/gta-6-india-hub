About

About UnlockIST

UnlockIST is a lightweight, unofficial fan utility site for India launch week: IST countdown, download-time math, rupee cost checklist, day planner, and edition quiz.

What we are not

Not Rockstar, Take-Two, PlayStation, or Xbox. No official logos, trailers, screenshots, proprietary fonts, or music. Visuals are original CSS and SVG.

Facts vs placeholders

Confirmed calendar target used here: Thursday, 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Exact unlock clock time, INR prices, and install size are treated as unconfirmed until you verify official sources. PC is not announced on this site’s last update.

Monetization

The site may show ads later, and some “Check price” buttons may use affiliate links with disclosure. Optional analytics may be added. See the privacy policy.
