About

About GTA 6 Hub

GTA 6 Hub is a lightweight, unofficial fan utility site: countdown and timezone conversion, a cautious edition quiz, a launch checklist, and a console setup comparison. It is built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript so it can deploy to Vercel or Netlify by dragging the folder — no backend required.

What we are not

We are not Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, PlayStation, or Xbox. We do not use official logos, trailer footage, screenshots, proprietary fonts, or soundtrack audio. Visuals on this site are original CSS and simple SVG marks.

Facts vs placeholders

Confirmed calendar target used here: Thursday, 19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Exact worldwide unlock clock time is treated as unconfirmed. Edition prices, collector contents, install sizes, and performance modes live in /data JSON with TODO notes until verified on official sources. A PC version is not announced on this site’s last update.

Monetization

The layout is AdSense-ready (hidden ad slots until a client ID is configured) and supports affiliate “Check price” links with disclosure. Analytics is optional via config.js. See the privacy policy for cookies and localStorage details.

Contact

Update contactEmail in config.js with a real address before launch. Operator context: indie site maintained for search traffic around launch week.
