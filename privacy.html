Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026. This is a static unofficial fan site. Replace contact details in config.js before going live.

Who we are

GTA 6 Hub is an unofficial fan utility website. It is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Contact: set contactEmail in config.js (placeholder: hello@example.com).

What we store in your browser

The launch checklist saves checkbox progress in localStorage on your device. That data never leaves your browser unless you clear site data. The countdown may remember a selected timezone only for the current page session (no account system).

Cookies & advertising (AdSense-ready)

Ad slots are reserved in the layout but stay hidden until adsenseClientId is set in config.js. When Google AdSense (or similar) is enabled, third-party cookies and advertising identifiers may be used to show personalized or non-personalized ads per your region’s consent rules. You should add a consent banner that matches your host country’s requirements (for example GDPR/ePrivacy if you serve EU visitors) before enabling ads.

Affiliate links

Some “Check price” buttons may use affiliate URLs from config.js. If you click those links and buy something, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate networks may set their own cookies.

Analytics

analyticsId in config.js is empty by default. If you add an analytics product later, it may collect page views, approximate location, device type, and referrers. Update this policy with the vendor name and opt-out link when you enable it.

Signup form

The home page signup only redirects or posts to the URL you configure. Until signupUrl points to a real provider, no email is collected by this static host.

Children

This site discusses an adult-rated game franchise. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

Changes

We may update this page as monetization tools are added. The date at the top will change when we do.
