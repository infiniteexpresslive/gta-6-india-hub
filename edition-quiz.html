How this quiz thinks

“Which edition to buy” is one of the highest-intent queries around a GTA launch. People usually trade off three things: lowest price, digital extras, and physical collector items. Our scoring weights those preferences using editable JSON — not scraped store pages — so you can update names the hour Rockstar publishes them.

The Check price button uses affiliate URLs from config.js with rel="sponsored noopener". Replace the placeholder links before promoting the page, and keep the disclosure visible. If a collector edition never materializes, remove it from editions.json so the quiz cannot recommend vaporware.

For platform fit (PS5 vs Series X vs Series S), use the setup check table. For day-one logistics, open the launch checklist.
